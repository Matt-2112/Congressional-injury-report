How statuses work

Out
Announced leave of absence, or missed every roll-call vote on the most recent vote day.
Injured Reserve
Missed every vote on three or more straight vote days with no announced return.
Questionable
Missed at least half of the latest day’s votes.
Probable
Back on the floor after sitting out a full vote day in the past week.

Built from official sources: House Clerk roll-call votes, Senate roll-call votes, the Congressional Record, and the congress-legislators project. Reasons are extracted from leave-of-absence grants and members’ personal explanations; when none is on record, the absence is listed as undisclosed.
